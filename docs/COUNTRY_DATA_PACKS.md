# Country Data Packs

Brovi Scan country packs are preparation guidance datasets, not legal advice.

## Active countries
Australia, Austria, Belgium, Canada, China, Czech Republic, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Japan, Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Poland, Spain, Sweden, Turkey, United Arab Emirates, United Kingdom, United States.

## Planned countries
South Korea, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Malaysia, Singapore, Russia, Switzerland, Portugal, Lithuania, Latvia, Estonia, Romania, Cyprus, Malta, South Africa, India.

## Rules
- Verify final requirements with official authorities and institutions.
- Do not treat outdated deadlines as current.
- Do not use guarantee wording.
- Use "may be required" for conditional documents.
- Activate planned countries only after official source verification.
- Update `lastReviewed` when content changes.


## Phase C expansion checklist
- Keep status as `planned` until official sources are validated.
- Add `researchTodo`, `officialSourceCandidates`, risk placeholders, and activation checklist before activation.
- Require legal/privacy review before handling sensitive country-specific evidence instructions.
- Avoid outdated data; re-check official pages and update `lastReviewed` when content changes.

## Source quality rules
- Prefer primary official sources (immigration authority, embassy/consulate, official university/government recognition portals).
- Use cautious wording such as "may be required" for conditional items.
- Never claim official completeness or guaranteed outcomes.
