# Country Data Packs

Brovi Scan country packs are preparation guidance datasets, not legal advice.

## Active countries
Australia, Austria, Canada, China, France, Germany, Italy, Japan, Netherlands, New Zealand, United Kingdom, United States.

## Planned countries
Spain, Belgium, Turkey, United Arab Emirates, Ireland, Sweden, Finland, Norway, Denmark, Poland, Czech Republic, Hungary, South Korea, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Malaysia, Singapore, Russia, Switzerland, Portugal, Lithuania, Latvia, Estonia, Romania, Cyprus, Malta, South Africa, India.

## Rules
- Verify final requirements with official authorities and institutions.
- Do not treat outdated deadlines as current.
- Do not use guarantee wording.
- Use "may be required" for conditional documents.
- Activate planned countries only after official source verification.
- Update `lastReviewed` when content changes.
